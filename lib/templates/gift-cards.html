Gift card questions

How do I send a gift?

Choose the amount, enter and confirm the recipient’s email, and select a send date. Your order shows the USD payment instructions when purchasing is available.

When can the recipient claim it?

After PURE confirms payment and the selected date arrives, the recipient can claim the gift here using a verified PURE account with the matching email address.

What if they don’t have an account?

They can create an account with the address you entered, verify their email and return to this page.

Can they spend part of it?

Claiming adds the gift amount to their PURE store credit. They can apply available credit at an eligible checkout and keep the rest for a later purchase.

Can I schedule a gift?

Choose today or a date up to 28 days ahead. Dates use UTC. The gift can be claimed once payment is confirmed and the date arrives.

What if I entered the wrong email?

Contact support@getpurelabs.com with the order reference and the correct address.

Can I use a coupon or account credit to buy a gift card?

Gift cards are funded separately by USD bank transfer for their full face value.
