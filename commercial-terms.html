Retail pricing and promotions

During a buy-one-get-one-free sale, each eligible unit you buy in the regular cart includes one matching free unit. Enter BLACKFRIDAY10 in the regular cart for 10% off the merchandise subtotal. The order summary shows the paid quantity, free units and applicable savings before purchase.

PURE WHITE members receive 5% off eligible retail merchandise and PURE BLACK members receive 10%. When a coupon also qualifies, checkout applies the larger saving. An eligible Crypto discount applies to the remaining retail merchandise balance when a Crypto payment is due. See the membership terms.

Bulk orders

In Bulk Orders, 10–49 units of a product qualify for 50% off and 50 or more qualify for 60% off. Strengths of the same product count together. Each product must qualify separately, and the tier applies to every unit of that product.

The bulk tier is the only merchandise discount on a bulk order. Buy-one-get-one offers, coupon and partner discounts, membership discounts and Crypto discounts apply only to eligible retail orders. US bulk orders include two-day express shipping after dispatch. Members earn points on the eligible amount paid.

Currency and monthly invoices

PURE sets prices in US dollars. Local currency amounts shown while browsing are estimates. Each Subscribe & Save invoice lists its total in USD. For Canadian Interac e-Transfers, send the CAD amount shown with your payment reference. PURE calculates that amount from the invoice total using the Bank of Canada USD/CAD rate available when you choose Interac and rounds to the nearest cent. The amount stays fixed for that invoice’s payment window.

PURE emails a new invoice each month while your plan is active. You choose how to pay each invoice. PURE never charges a saved card automatically. Pause, skip or cancel future invoices from your account.

Checkout and payment

For eligible retail orders, check out as a guest with your email address or sign in. Sign in for bulk orders and account benefits. Review your total and available payment methods before placing your order. Use the exact amount and order reference in the payment instructions. Payment remains pending until verified receipt or authenticated provider confirmation.

Cards and digital wallets appear only when the configured provider account supports them. Any card-to-crypto route has a separate disclosure and provider confirmation. Any applicable payment processing charge is shown before payment.

For a guest order, keep your PURE order email and order number. To resume a pending payment, open the order link in the same browser you used at checkout. If the link is unavailable or you need help, include the order number and checkout email when you contact support.

Shipping and fulfillment

US express and Canadian standard shipping are free when eligible merchandise totals at least US$250 after applicable discounts, before points or store credit. Below that threshold, standard shipping is US$11.95 within the United States and US$24.95 within Canada. Canadian Interac eligibility is evaluated using the same underlying USD amount, with the payment total converted to CAD at the checkout exchange rate.

Active PURE WHITE and PURE BLACK memberships include standard or express shipping to US addresses. PURE BLACK also includes priority fulfillment. Express delivery without an included membership benefit, next-day service, international shipping and optional shipment protection are separately priced at checkout. Destination availability, carrier coverage, customs and import charges may affect delivery. Included priority fulfillment does not guarantee an arrival date.

Fulfillment is arranged after confirmed payment. After dispatch, check your shipment email for carrier and tracking details when available. If you ordered while signed in, you can also find them under Track Order. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. See the shipping policy.

The payment window is normally 24 hours. Do not pay an expired or cancelled order. If no payment has been sent, cancel an unpaid account order to release reserved balances. If funds have already been sent or a payment attempt is pending, contact support for reconciliation. An order that cannot be fulfilled may be cancelled and refunded.

Refunds and payment evidence

Refunds are performed through the original approved bank or provider route where supported and then reconciled to the order. Recording a refund in the admin system does not itself move money. Customer rights and the published refund policy continue to apply. Unrecognized, duplicate, late or mismatched payments require reconciliation before fulfillment.

Points and store credit

Existing issued balances remain available. On eligible paid product orders, active PURE WHITE earns 5 points per US$1 and PURE BLACK earns 10 points per US$1. Earnings use merchandise after discounts and any points or store-credit redemptions; fractional points are rounded down. Points are issued after payment is verified. CAD Interac orders earn points using the underlying USD merchandise value after discounts. A free account does not earn points on new orders.

Each point is worth US$0.01 toward merchandise, with a minimum redemption of 100 points. Issued USD store credit may also pay delivery charges in USD checkout. USD points and store credit cannot be redeemed against a CAD payment or a membership fee. Balances cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. No automatic expiry applies.

PURE BLACK receives US$50 store credit for a future order after a qualifying paid product order reaches at least US$150 in eligible merchandise after discounts and before points or credit redemptions. This credit is issued at most once per UTC calendar month and does not reduce the qualifying order's total. CAD orders use their underlying USD merchandise value for qualification.

Redemptions are reserved when an order is placed. Cancelling an unpaid order restores them. A completed full refund restores eligible redeemed points and credit according to the recorded order. Product refunds reverse the related earned points and trigger review or reversal of any qualifying monthly credit. Historical earned points are reconciled under the terms recorded with that purchase.
